# deskgit 0.8.0

deskgit now works with DeskTick.

- **Sign in again after updating**: deskgit signs in through your browser on DeskTick, the same way the desktop app does. Your old sign-in is not carried over; start the sign-in from inside deskgit and confirm in the browser
- **Tasks, projects and the timer come from DeskTick**: the task picker, "new task", moving a task, linked repositories and the running timer all use your DeskTick workspace
- **A new look**: panels have corner brackets and the DeskTick yellow accent, and the status panel shows your day at a glance (time today, time this week, the task you are on)
- The site defaults to `https://dev.desktick.com`; set `desktimers.url` in your config, or `DESKTICK_URL`, to point somewhere else
